Who we are

Your home by the sea in Igalo

Opposite Blatna Plaža in Igalo, 100 metres from the sea and 300 metres from the Institut Dr Simo Milošević, you will find the Monako studio apartments. Our guests come here for the quiet, the calm and the domestic feel that makes it possible to actually rest from everyday stress. The accommodation suits families with children, but works equally well for couples and small groups.

There is an IDEA supermarket inside the building, which covers everything you need day to day without leaving the door.

Guest reviews and impressions matter to us. We try to meet every expectation and to make each stay worth recommending - which is reflected in our ratings on Google, Booking.com and Airbnb.

Every unit has a balcony with a direct sea view. All are air conditioned and equipped with a TV and cable channels, WiFi and parking. Daily cleaning and changes of towels and bed linen are part of our standard service.

Igalo is a tourist town in its own right - seafood, therapeutic mud, the promenade, restaurants and excursions are all a short walk from your door. And Igalo lives in winter too: every February the square a five-minute walk from our apartments becomes the main stage of the Mimosa Festival, which we have followed at first hand since it began.

Our obligation is to give guests quality service and every kind of information they need. Our decades of experience in tourism, and the satisfaction of our guests, are our recommendation.
