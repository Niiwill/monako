## 3. Changes made (deploy #6, PR #71)

Meta-only truncation fixes. No titles, visible content, schema or NAP touched.

**`blatna-plaza.html`** — meta 167 → 130 chars. Google was cutting the copy
before "Apartmani Monako su samo 100 m od plaže", removing the conversion
hook. New version surfaces the intents that already convert on this page
(cijene, smještaj, mapa, lekovito blato). Also drops the absolute medical
claim "radioaktivnom mulju koji liječi reumatizam" in favour of "lekovito
blato" — the term the page body and the real queries use, and a safer claim.

**`vesti/beaches-herceg-novi.html`** — meta 177 → 125 chars, was truncating
mid-sentence.

Expected impact: **+30–60 clicks/28d** combined. Deliberately modest — these
are snippet-completeness fixes, not repositioning.
