## 2. New surface discovered: the GBP-tagged homepage URL

`https://apartmani-igalo.com/?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=organic` appeared in
the Pages report for the first time on **Jul 25 only**: 155 impressions,
0 clicks, average position 3.1.

Its queries are the money terms, at remarkable positions:

| Query | Impr | Pos |
|---|---|---|
| apartmani igalo | 27 | **1.67** |
| apartman igalo | 3 | **1.00** |
| apartmani monako igalo | 3 | **1.00** |
| apartmani igalo izdavanje | 4 | 3.00 |

**Diagnosis — this is not a duplicate-content problem.** URL Inspection
returns *"URL is unknown to Google"* (not indexed, no Google-selected
canonical). These are **Google Business Profile website-button / local-pack
impressions**, which GSC attributes to whatever URL the profile publishes —
and the profile's website field carries the UTM string. The audit's
conclusion on item G8 ("no change needed, canonical resolves it") stands.

Two useful consequences:

1. **It partly explains Saturday's homepage dip.** Homepage impressions on
   Jul 25 were 402 vs 649 the previous Saturday — but 155 impressions moved
   to the UTM URL. Combined: 557. The site total is unaffected; only the
   per-page attribution split.
2. **It is direct evidence of local-pack strength** — position 1.0–1.67 on
   the money queries, which the ordinary blue-link homepage does not reach.
   The GBP work is paying off in a surface the website cannot reach.

Note: clicks on that button are counted in **GBP's** "Website clicks" (94 in
the last export), not in GSC — hence 0 clicks here. Nothing is being lost.

Reporting caveat going forward: homepage page-level metrics will understate
reality whenever the local pack shows. I will report both lines when the UTM
URL has volume.
