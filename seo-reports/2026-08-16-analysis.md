## 7. Method note

Two calibration lessons landed on the same day, pulling in opposite
directions.

Yesterday I led with a brand-query "alert" that turned out to be two days of
noise on a query drawing 6–22 impressions daily. Today the atrakcije test —
where I did pre-register thresholds — is quietly heading toward rejecting my
own diagnosis.

The difference is that one had a stated threshold and the other did not. The
rule I am adding:

> **State the sample size that would make a movement real before calling it.**
> On low-volume queries, two days of position change is noise by default; the
> burden is to show otherwise, not to flag first and check later.
