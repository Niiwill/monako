| | Pre | Post |

### What I checked before calling it

**Composition.** The `herceg novi` head term re-entered hard: 60 impressions at
position 25.4 before, **233 at 12.40** after. Removing it, page position goes
**5.50 → 5.91** instead of 6.24 → 7.14. So two thirds of the page-level
decline is that one term returning. The cluster figures above already exclude
it.

**Season.** Site clicks fell 28.7% across the same boundary; the atrakcije
page fell 43.5% and the cluster's impressions fell 49%. It fell roughly twice
as fast as the site.

**Sample.** 77 impressions post-change is thin — just above my own 50-impression
floor. A position of 4.40 on 77 impressions is weak evidence on its own. But
it is weak evidence pointing the same way as the page-level data, the
impression collapse, and the CTR that never moved.

### The honest conclusion

I removed PR #76's three orientation sections to refocus the page on the
cluster that earns. **The cluster is worse than before the removal.** Position
lost 0.92, impressions halved, CTR did not move at all.

That makes **four** of four attempts this season to improve a page that was
already working: PR #76 itself, the meta reverts, the image sitemap, and now
the refocus. The rule written on Aug 19 has now been tested four times and
held four times.

**No further change to this page.** Not a re-revert, not an adjustment. The
page is left exactly as it is and comes off the active list.

## 3. ⭐ Two broken rich results, found by inspection

### `monthly-rental-igalo.html` — verdict FAIL

| Issue | Severity | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Rating is outside the specified or default range | **ERROR** | 2 |
| Missing field `itemReviewed` | **ERROR** | 3 |
| Invalid object type for field `author` | WARNING | 3 |

Google reports **Review snippets: FAIL** on this page. It also has not been
crawled since **2026-07-09** — 55 days, the stalest page on the property.

Two things make this worth acting on. First, invalid Review markup is a
structured-data error Google explicitly rejects. Second, the standing entity
invariant is that there is **no `aggregateRating` or `Review` on the business
node** — this page carries Review markup with an out-of-range rating, which
is both broken and against the rule we set.

**I have not touched it.** It needs a decision: strip the Review markup
entirely, or repair it. Stripping is my recommendation and is consistent with
the invariant, but that is a call on business markup, not a data fix.

### `nova-godina-herceg-novi.html` — Events PASS, 3 warnings

All three `MusicEvent` items report **Missing field `endDate`**. The rich
result still passes, so this is not urgent — but the page enters its decisive
season in six weeks, and an event without an end date is a real gap in the
markup. Cheap to fix, no downside I can see.

## 9. Method note

Three pre-registered tests came due while nobody was reading, and all three
were answerable the moment access returned. That is the whole value of writing
thresholds down before the data exists: a ten-day gap cost nothing except ten
days.

The atrakcije result is the one worth sitting with. It was my change, argued
for on the strength of a seasonal control, shipped with a clean test — and it
made the page worse. The test caught it in eight days. Without the thresholds
I would have read "position 4.40, still respectable" and moved on.

> **Four of four attempts to improve a working page have failed this season.**
> The next such proposal needs a reason why it differs from these four, stated
> before it ships.
