### Candidate causes, in rough order of suspicion

1. **Removal of `aggregateRating`/`review` from the business nodes** (audit
   item C2). Policy-correct and must stay — self-serving review markup cannot
   produce stars and was a liability — but it did remove entity signal.
2. **`starRating: 3` added** (item G2). Accurate per the site's own copy, but
   it does newly declare a mid-tier rating to Google.
3. **Schema type changed back to `LodgingBusiness`** (item M2) — the second
   type flip in nine days; each one resets entity understanding.
4. **UI overhaul body/layout changes** to `index.html`.
5. Not ruled out: seasonality, the UTM/GBP URL absorbing signal, or an
   unannounced Google update.

**Recommendation: watch, do not revert anything yet.** The C2 removal is not
reversible on policy grounds regardless. If the homepage has not recovered
toward ~6 by **Aug 5**, the next step is a surgical schema diff of `index.html`
between Jul 24 and now — not a blanket rollback.

rest on **4 clicks** in the post window — inside noise. Page-level, with
4,944 impressions, is the reliable read.

**Formal verdict still Aug 3**, and it must be position-adjusted rather than
a raw CTR comparison, because position moved for unrelated reasons.
