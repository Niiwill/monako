## 2. Changes made (deploy #4)

### Change 1 — `vesti/10-razloga-da-posetite-igalo.html`

- **Target queries:** "igalo" (6,905 impr, pos 6.9, 1.9% CTR), "igalo crna
  gora" (273), "igalo herceg novi" (227), "igalo iskustva" (116, 6.0% CTR),
  "igalo montenegro" (247).
- **Issue (data):** the site's single biggest query by impressions converts
  at 1.9% against ~4% expected for position 6.9 (~187 missed clicks/28d).
  Old title was 71 chars — truncated in SERP — and buried the exact query
  term at character 28 behind "10 Razloga da Posjetite". Old meta 199 chars,
  also truncated, losing its payoff.
- **Change:** lead with the query term + region, retain the "10 Razloga"
  promise (keeps consistency with H1/og:title/schema headline so Google is
  less likely to rewrite), surface the highest-CTR sub-intents (plaže,
  blato, iskustva, cijene).

```diff
- <title>10 Razloga da Posjetite Igalo i Herceg Novi – Vodič 2026 | Monako Igalo</title>   (71 chars)
- <meta name="description" content="Tražite savršeno mjesto za odmor? Otkrijte 10 razloga zašto Igalo i Herceg Novi trebaju biti vaša sljedeća destinacija — prelepe plaže, lekovito blato, bogata istorija, odlična hrana i povoljne cijene.">   (199 chars)
+ <title>Igalo, Crna Gora – 10 Razloga za Odmor: Plaže i Blato 2026</title>   (58 chars)
+ <meta name="description" content="Igalo kod Herceg Novog: plaže, lekovito blato, šetalište i cijene. Iskustva gostiju i 10 razloga zašto Igalo vrijedi posjetiti u 2026.">   (134 chars)
```

- **Expected impact:** CTR 1.9% → 3–4% on "igalo" ≈ **+70–140 clicks/28d**;
  possible secondary position gain from tighter query-title match.

### Change 2 — `vesti/restorani-herceg-novi.html`

- **Target queries:** "restorani herceg novi" (350, pos 5.1), "herceg novi
  restorani" (370, pos 5.3), "restoran herceg novi" (135, pos 5.6).
- **Issue (data):** page has 10,466 impr at 2.43% CTR / pos 6.8 (expected

- **Expected impact:** CTR 2.43% → 3.0–3.5% ≈ **+60–110 clicks/28d**.
