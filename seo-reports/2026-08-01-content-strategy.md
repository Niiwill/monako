# Content Strategy — 5 new pages from one month of GSC data

**Data:** 1,900 distinct queries, 65,987 impressions, 2026-07-01 → 2026-07-28.
**Key context:** **1,486 queries (10,520 impressions) produced zero clicks.**
That is the raw material below.

## The strategic problem this solves

Weekly clicks have plateaued: 867 → 1,909 → 2,002 → 2,020 → 2,017. Summer
beach demand has peaked and will now decline. Every high-performing page on
the site is a **summer** page. Four of the five proposals below target
**year-round or off-season** demand deliberately — that is how the site keeps
growing when the beaches empty.

Note on sizing: GSC only reports queries the site **already ranks for**. The
true demand for these themes is larger than the numbers below, which are
therefore floors, not ceilings.

---

## #1 — Banja Igalo / Institut "Dr Simo Milošević" ⭐ highest value

**File:** `vesti/banja-igalo-institut.html`

| Theme total | 1,536 impressions | 8.6% CTR |
|---|---|---|

**The finding: "Simo Milošević" appears on ZERO pages of the site.** Igalo's
most famous institution — the thalassotherapy institute the town is known for
across the region — is not named anywhere. Meanwhile:

| Query | Impr | Pos | Clicks |
|---|---|---|---|
| banja igalo | 67 | 6.2 | **1** |
| plaza instituta dr simo milosevic | 50 | 6.1 | 2 |
| plaža instituta dr simo milošević | 43 | 5.9 | 1 |
| stara banja beach | 42 | 2.2 | 1 |
| stara banja igalo | 160 | 2.4 | 6 |

The medical/spa intent currently lands on `blatna-plaza.html` — a **beach**
page. Someone researching rehabilitation or thalassotherapy gets a page about
sunbeds. That mismatch is why "banja igalo" converts at 1 click from 67
impressions.

**Why this is the top priority: it is year-round.** Medical and rehabilitation
tourism runs September through June — precisely when beach traffic dies. This
is the single best answer to the seasonal plateau, and it maps directly to a
high-value guest: longer stays, off-season, booked in advance.

**Cover:** what the Institute is and treats, the difference between Stara Banja
and Blatna plaža, how the lekovito blato is used, who comes and when, what a
stay near the Institute looks like, walking distance from the apartments.
**Do not invent** treatment prices, medical claims or admission procedures.

## #2 — Kafići i barovi u Herceg Novom i Igalu

**File:** `vesti/kafici-barovi-herceg-novi-igalo.html`

| Theme total | 383 impressions | **2 clicks — 0.5% CTR** |
|---|---|---|

The worst-converting theme on the site, and the SERP is weak — the same
pattern that made the La Bamba section worth adding.

| Query | Impr | Pos | Clicks |
|---|---|---|---|
| tropik lounge bar herceg novi | 69 | 9.6 | 0 |
| holiday lounge bar (3 variants incl. menu, reviews) | 106 | 6.8–8.5 | 0 |
| herceg novi kafici | 46 | 9.2 | 0 |
| spada bar herceg novi | 35 | 8.9 | 1 |
| igalo kafici / kafici igalo / kafici herceg novi | 58 | 6.0–9.9 | 1 |
| belve bar igalo, plazni bar igalo, banja bar | 26 | 7.4–10.9 | 0 |

People are searching **named venues** with **menu** and **review** intent and
finding nothing usable. Holiday Lounge Bar already appears on the restaurants
page as a restaurant — but the searches treat it as a bar.

**Seasonal fit:** shoulder season. Café culture in Herceg Novi runs well past
summer.

## #5 — Temperatura mora u Igalu i sezona kupanja

**File:** `vesti/temperatura-mora-igalo.html`

| Theme total | 164 impressions | **0 clicks — 0.0%** |
|---|---|---|

| Query | Impr | Pos |
|---|---|---|
| temperatura mora igalo | 80 | 9.4 |
| igalo temperatura mora | 44 | 8.8 |
| temperatura mora u igalu | 18 | 8.9 |
| + 11 variants | 22 | 9.0–12.0 |

Plus the adjacent `igalo vrijeme` — **297 impressions at position 12.0 with
zero clicks**, the largest single zero-click query on the site.

Every one of these sits at position 8–12 and earns nothing. They are
**recurring, year-round, planning-stage** queries — people check sea
temperature months before booking. A month-by-month table with the seasonal
context the site already documents (22–26 °C summer, ~240 sunny days, mild
10–18 °C winters) would answer them directly.

Partly touched by PR #76's weather section, but that is a paragraph inside an
attractions guide — not a page that can win a temperature query.

| 5 | Temperatura mora | 164 (+297) | 0 | +25–50 | **year-round** |

**Combined realistic: +185–360 clicks per 28 days**, roughly a 3–5% lift on
current volume — but weighted toward months when the current content earns
nothing.

Sequence: **#1 first** (biggest, and the off-season clock is running — it needs
to rank before September). Then #5 and #4 (cheap, year-round), then #2 and #3.

## Requirement for all five

Every page needs **verifiable local detail**. Three of these subjects — the
Institute, Villa Galeb, Žanjice — are real places with real facts I must not
invent. Each should be written in an implementation session with instructions
to use only what can be confirmed, and to describe qualitatively where a
specific figure is uncertain. That constraint is also what will make them beat
the thin aggregator pages currently ranking.
