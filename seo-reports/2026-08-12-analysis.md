### What every one of these edits did

They traded named, concrete detail for shorter generic wording:

- `restorani-igalo` lost *Šeki Nautilus i Stara Hercegovina* and
  *udaljenost od Blatne plaže*
- `restorani-herceg-novi` lost *od konoba uz Blatnu plažu i Promenade u
  Igalu do fine dining Verige 65* → *konobe, riba i fine dining*

  Luštica*
- the bike pages lost *(ljetnja zabrana 19–01 h, **kazna 100 €**)*

The named restaurant, the named beach, the actual fine — those are the
reasons someone clicks. The stated goal was to stop SERP truncation, which is
cosmetic. The cost was clicks.

clicks/week** if the old snippet restores it.

## 7. Method note

Yesterday's investigation produced a reusable test, and running it the next
morning turned a single-page fix into a five-page one. The test is cheap:

> For every page, compare CTR and average position across aligned weeks.
> **Position flat or better with CTR materially down is never a ranking
> problem** — it is the snippet, a SERP feature, or a lost rich result.

Worth adding to the standing daily run. It would have caught the
`restorani-herceg-novi` decline on **Jul 26**, two weeks and roughly 30
clicks/week earlier than it was actually caught.
