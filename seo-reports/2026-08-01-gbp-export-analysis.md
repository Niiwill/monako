**Messaging went from zero to 36 conversations in 25 days.** That was the top

conversation that previously had no way to happen.

Note: the individual channels sum to 383 while Google reports 347
"interactions" — Google de-duplicates, so treat interactions as its own
series rather than a total.

## 2. ⚠ The +19% on calls is **not** attributable to the NAP work

The two exports overlap heavily (Jul 3–23 sits in both) and cover different
parts of the season — the previous window included late June, which is
lower-demand. So the increases in calls, directions and website clicks are
best read as **seasonal**, not as evidence of any change we made.

The only clean, unconfounded result here is messaging, because it went from a
channel that did not exist to one that does.

## 3. The honest NAP read — no conversion lift yet

The GBP landing URL appeared in Search on **Jul 25**. This export gives three
days after that:

| Metric | Jul 3–24 (per day) | Jul 25–27 (per day) |
|---|---|---|
| Interactions | 13.86 | 14.00 |
| Calls | 6.32 | **5.33** |
| Directions | 3.68 | **7.00** |
| Website clicks | 3.86 | **1.67** |
| Messages | 1.50 | 1.00 |

**The extra search visibility has not yet turned into more profile
conversions.** Website clicks actually fell in those three days, and calls
were slightly down. Directions jumped, but on three days that is noise.

Last 7 usable days vs the 7 before: interactions −3%, calls +5%, directions
+6%, website clicks −22%, messages −62%.

### So: is NAP showing results?

**Split answer, and both halves matter.**

- **In Search visibility: yes, clearly.** The GBP URL went from 0 to 1,151
  impressions in four days, at position 2.3–3.6, ranking 1.2–1.6 for
  non-brand commercial queries. Combined homepage + GBP impressions rose 12%.
- **In profile conversions: not yet.** Three days of GBP data show no lift,
  and one channel moved down.

That is not a contradiction — it is a normal lag between *being seen* and
*being contacted*, and three days cannot resolve it. **The real test is the
next export**, which should cover a full two weeks after Jul 25.

I would rather state this plainly than let the earlier "NAP is paying off"
framing stand unqualified: the visibility gain is measured, the revenue gain
is not yet.

## 4. Remaining gap: Bookings still zero

Messaging closed the biggest hole. **Bookings is now the only channel still
reading zero.** With the GBP URL winning `apartmani igalo cijene` (pos 1.20)
and `privatni smestaj igalo i cene` (pos 2.50), the arriving intent is
explicitly about price and availability — a booking link pointed at
`/#cjenovnik` would meet it directly.

## 5. Message-handling note

36 conversations in 25 days is roughly 1.4 per day, and it is a channel where
response time is itself a ranking and conversion factor — Google surfaces
responsiveness, and guests comparing several properties tend to book whoever
answers first. Worth setting an auto-reply with realistic expectations if one
is not already in place.
