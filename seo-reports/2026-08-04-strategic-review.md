# Strategic Review — what the last two months actually produced

**Data:** GSC 28d windows, 2026-07-06 → 2026-08-02 vs 2026-06-08 → 2026-07-05.
This is an analysis, not a status report. It changes the recommended direction.

---

## 1. The finding that matters

Split every query into **commercial intent** (apartman, smještaj, izdavanje,
najam, cijene, rezervacija, soba, privatni) and **informational** (everything
else), then compare the two 28-day windows:

| Intent | Previous 28d | This 28d | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Informational | 1,481 clicks | 3,870 clicks | **+161%** |
| **Commercial** | **420 clicks** | **410 clicks** | **−2%** |

**Total traffic nearly tripled. Traffic that can book an apartment did not
move.** Every click of growth in the last two months was informational.

Break the commercial half down further:

| | Clicks | Impressions | CTR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand (`monako …`) | 197 | 911 | **21.6%** |
| **Non-brand** | **213** | **9,317** | **2.3%** |

**New-customer acquisition from organic search is ~7.6 clicks/day**, and that
figure is flat across both windows. The brand half is people who already know
the business — they would have found it anyway.

---

## 2. Three structural facts behind it

### a) 63% of the site is one beach article

| Page | Clicks | Share |
|---|---|---|
| `vesti/plaze-herceg-novi.html` | 4,948 | **63.1%** |
| `/` (the money page) | 686 | 8.7% |
| `blatna-plaza.html` | 565 | 7.2% |
| `atrakcije-herceg-novi` | 494 | 6.3% |
| `10-razloga` | 442 | 5.6% |
| `restorani-herceg-novi` | 385 | 4.9% |

The homepage — the only page that sells anything — receives **8.7%** of site
traffic. This is a tourism magazine with a rental business attached, not the
other way round.

### b) 76% of clicks are summer-dependent

Queries containing plaža / more / kupanje / beach / ležaljke / temperatura
account for **3,248 of 4,280 query-level clicks (76%)**. Beach demand does not
decline gently in September — it falls off a cliff. On current composition the
site should expect to lose most of its traffic within six weeks, and the part
it keeps is the part that was never growing.

### c) Commercial rankings are stuck in a narrow band

| Position band | Commercial impressions | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Top 3 | 1,474 | 14.4% |
| **4–10** | **8,754** | **85.6%** |
| 10+ | 0 | 0.0% |

This is the most diagnostic number in the whole dataset. Nothing is beyond
position 10 — coverage is not the problem. **86% of commercial demand sits at
positions 4–10**, converting at 2.3%, when top-3 would convert at 8–14%.

The band is not accidental. Positions 1–3 on `igalo apartmani`,
`apartmani igalo`, `igalo smjestaj` are held by Booking, Airbnb and the
aggregator directories. A domain with **authority score 10 and 123 referring
domains** does not outrank them with better copy. That ceiling is authority,
and no amount of on-page work moves it.

---

## 3. Where I have been wrong

I have spent three weeks optimising titles and meta descriptions, then
writing five new articles. Judged honestly:

- The meta work produced real CTR gains on **informational** pages.
- `herceg novi plaze` reaching position 1.6 at 14.2% CTR is a genuine win — it
  is also **the single largest contributor to the concentration problem**, and
  it sells nothing directly.
- Four of the five new articles target informational or seasonal intent. They
  are good pages. They are **more of the thing that was already working and
  already not converting.**

The one exception is the banja/Institut page, which targets off-season demand
from people who book multi-week stays. That was the right instinct; it should
have been the whole batch, not one fifth of it.

**Correction to my own advice:** I framed the content plan as "+185–360
clicks/28d." Measured against what the business needs, most of that is more
informational traffic. The right metric was never total clicks.

---

## 4. What the data says to do instead

### The prize, quantified

8,754 commercial impressions sit at positions 4–10, converting at 2.3%
(~190 clicks). At realistic top-3 CTR for this market (~11%):

| Scenario | Commercial clicks/28d |
|---|---|
| Today | ~190 non-brand |
| Same impressions at top-3 | **~960** |

**That is +770 new-customer clicks per 28 days — a 4× increase in acquisition
— without a single new page.** For comparison, all five new articles combined
target 185–360, mostly informational.

### But the blue-link route is blocked

Reaching top-3 organically means beating Booking and Airbnb on their own
terms. At AS 10 / 123 referring domains, that is a 12–24 month authority
project, not a content project.

### The route that is already working

The GBP landing URL now sits at **position 2.47** on exactly these commercial
queries — `apartmani igalo` (1.61), `apartmani igalo blizu plaze` (1.29),
`apartmani igalo cijene` (1.20) — while the organic homepage sits at 3.2–4.4
for the same terms.

**The local pack is already delivering the top-3 commercial position the blue
links cannot reach.** And it is compounding: GBP-URL clicks went 13 → 25 week
over week, impressions 1,781 → 2,208.

This reframes the whole programme. For a single-property rental competing
against aggregators, **the local pack is the commercial channel and the
website is the credibility layer that supports it.**

---

## 5. Revised priorities

**1. Local pack first — it is the only channel where top-3 is achievable.**
Reviews, photos, posts, Q&A, attribute completeness. The Jul 3–27 export shows
messaging went 0 → 36 conversations once enabled; bookings remain at 0. These
are the levers that move the channel that is actually converting.

**2. Fix the funnel on the traffic that already exists.**
The beach article carries 63% of site traffic and its **first link to the
homepage sits at 65% article depth** — past where most mobile readers stop.
`blatna-plaza` first links at 27%, `atrakcije` at 9%. Moving a contextual
"gdje odsjesti" link into the first third of `plaze-herceg-novi` is a one-line
change against 4,948 clicks. This is the highest-leverage on-site edit
available and I have not done it.

**3. Off-season content, not more summer content.**
76% summer exposure is a business risk, not just a metric. The banja page is
the correct model: year-round demand, longer stays, booked in advance.

**4. Authority, patiently.**
123 referring domains is the real ceiling on commercial rankings. A handful of
quality local links — Herceg Novi tourism portal, Montenegro travel media —
is worth more than another article. This is slow and unglamorous and it is
the only thing that eventually unblocks positions 1–3.

**5. Stop optimising for total clicks.**
From now on I will report **non-brand commercial clicks** as the headline
metric. Today's baseline: **213 per 28 days, 7.6/day.** Total clicks will keep
falling as summer ends and that is fine — it is not the number that pays.

---

## 6. Immediate next action

The funnel fix on `plaze-herceg-novi.html`. It is one contextual link into the
top third of the page that carries 63% of the site's traffic, and it is the
only item on this list that is both high-leverage and entirely within my
control. I will prepare it as a separate PR against a locked baseline
(homepage 686 clicks, 8.7% of site traffic) so its effect is measurable.
