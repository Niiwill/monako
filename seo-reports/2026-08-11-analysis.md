## Executive summary

The first weekly click decline of the programme (**−4.4% WoW**) arrived this
week. It is **not** the seasonal cliff — summer demand rose in all four
weeks. The dip is concentrated in non-beach informational queries and is
within normal variance.

The finding that matters is structural, and it changes when work should be
scheduled: **commercial and informational demand peak in different months.**
Commercial search peaked in July and is now falling while beach search is at
its highest of the year. **The booking window is April–June** — by August the
audience is already on holiday.

Commercial *performance* is healthy: clicks 335 → 337, CTR 3.50% → **3.66%**,
position 4.86 → 4.98. The impression decline is demand, not ranking.

## 2. ⭐ Key insight: commercial and informational demand peak in different months

Impressions per day, by intent, month by month:

| Month | Commercial | Summer/beach | Ratio C:S |
|---|---|---|---|
| April | 33 | 85 | 0.39 |
| **May** | 181 | 177 | **1.02** |
| June | 335 | 453 | 0.74 |
| **July** | **360** ← peak | 803 | 0.45 |
| Aug (first 7d) | 324 ↓ | **858** ← peak | 0.38 |

Three things follow:

1. **Commercial demand peaked in July and is now declining.** People book
   before they travel; by August they have booked.
2. **Informational demand is still climbing** and has not peaked. The
   September cliff has not started.
3. **The audience is most booking-focused in May** (ratio 1.02) — that is
   when accommodation search is at parity with beach search.

**Commercial demand grew 10× between April and July** (33 → 360/day). The
window where that growth is captured is **April–June**, which means rankings
and content must already be in place by **March**.

### What this changes

- **Optimising commercial pages now is optimising for a season that has
  already been bought.** The work still matters — but it is being banked for
  next April, not for this August.
- **The authority project has a deadline.** 123 referring domains is the
  ceiling on commercial rankings; that needs to move before the April–June
  surge, not during it.
- **The off-season content bet is validated.** Beach demand will collapse in
  autumn; the banja/Institut page targets Sept–June health tourism, which is
  the only demand that persists.
