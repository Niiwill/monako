## Addendum — gap B implemented after all (PR #75)

The owner questioned holding the distance FAQ, and they were right: the
caution in §3B was overweighted. Two things undercut it.

1. **FAQ rich results will not render here.** Google restricted them to
   authoritative government and health sites in 2023. An `FAQPage` block on
   a small travel site therefore does not change SERP appearance — which is
   precisely what the deploy-#5 CTR test measures. Title and meta are
   untouched, so the tested variable is unchanged.
2. **A separate branch puts merge timing in the owner's hands.** Shipping the
   work and letting them decide when to merge is strictly better than not
   writing it.

Implemented in **PR #75**: one FAQ entry answering "Koliko je Igalo daleko
od Herceg Novog?", using only figures already stated on the site and
consistent across pages (3 km to the centre, 4 km to Stari grad, Pet Danica
promenade). Target: ~590 impressions at ~1% CTR.

Verification also surfaced a **pre-existing** schema/visible mismatch — the
JSON-LD asked "od Dubrovnika?" while the page displayed "od aerodroma
Dubrovnik?". FAQ markup must mirror visible content, so the JSON-LD was
corrected. All five questions now match the page exactly.

Residual risk, stated plainly: body content can shift ranking slightly. If a
perfectly clean deploy-#5 read matters more than ~10 days of this
opportunity, PR #75 can sit until Aug 9.

**Gap A (Stari Grad, 970 impr) remains queued** — it needs a new page with
genuine local knowledge, which belongs in an implementation session rather
than being fabricated here. Prompt is in §5.
