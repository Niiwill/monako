## 7. Method note

Yesterday's rule earned its place on its first day. The New Year page produced
a −7.9 point CTR signature — exactly the shape that drove the July meta
reverts, which cost five page edits and were formally rejected on Aug 18. One
recomputation showed the named half of the page moving in the opposite
direction, and one crawl-date check exonerated the only recent change.

Both of today's dismissals came from the same discipline: **before explaining a
movement, check whether the set that produced it is the same set.**

The standing run now reads:

> 1. Print the impressions behind any quoted position; under ~50 it is not a
>    measurement.
> 2. The sample test applies to each end of a comparison, not the span.
> 3. Before reporting a change in average position or CTR, recompute it
>    without entries that joined or left the set.
> 4. A change Google has not crawled is unshipped, not failed — and a change
>    sitting on an unmerged branch is not even that.
