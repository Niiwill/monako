Impressions also **rose** (8 → 19 on the homepage) while positions fell. More
impressions at worse positions, with the whole block shifting uniformly, is
the signature of **something being inserted above us on that SERP** — a
competitor, an aggregator, or an expanded hotel/local module — not of a
page-level problem.

That argues against our own changes. But eight commits landed on `index.html`
on the evening of **Aug 10** (entity consolidation, Airbnb in `sameAs`, FAQ
markup, price removal), and the break is the very next day. I cannot separate
the two from GSC alone.

**Containment:** the aggregate is still healthy. Across all 14 brand queries,
position 1.03 → 2.39, CTR 22.78% → 19.79%, clicks **59 → 56**. Every other
brand variant — `monako apartmani igalo`, `monako igalo`, `apartman monako
igalo` — is still at position 1.00. The damage is one variant, and it is
small so far.

**Action:** re-check in 2–3 days. If it persists, do a manual SERP inspection
for that query to see what occupies the top slots. Do not change `index.html`
in the meantime — that would destroy the ability to tell the two hypotheses
apart.

### Pre-registered test, to be read on Aug 18 (7 days post-recrawl)

- **CTR ≥ 4.5%** → the meta rewrite is confirmed as the cause; proceed to
  chase recrawls on the remaining four pages.
- **CTR ≤ 3.6%** → the meta hypothesis is **rejected**. The diagnosis that
  drove five page reverts would be wrong, and the real cause is elsewhere.
- Between the two → inconclusive, extend to 14 days.

Writing the thresholds down now so the result cannot be rationalised after
the fact.

## 7. Method note

Yesterday's rule earned its place immediately: checking `lastCrawlTime` first
turned today's "the fix still isn't working" into "the fix has had two days,
which is nothing."

Today adds the complementary discipline. Where yesterday's risk was reading a
result too early, today's is reading one *conveniently* — a null result on an
intervention I recommended is exactly the result I would be tempted to
explain away. Hence the pre-registered thresholds in section 3, written
before the data exists.
