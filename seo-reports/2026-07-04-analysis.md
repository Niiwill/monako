## What's happening

1. **Seasonal lift + genuine ranking gains.** June growth is partly summer-season demand, but positions improved across the board (avg 7.5 → 6.4), so this is not seasonality alone. Daily clicks climbed from ~50-70/day (early June) to a stable 110-140/day plateau since ~June 14.
2. **The beach cluster is the traffic engine.** `/vesti/plaze-herceg-novi.html` earns 1,766 clicks (57% of all clicks), more than doubling period-over-period (809 → 1,766, pos 6.2 → 5.5). `/blatna-plaza.html` also surged (194 → 474 clicks, holding pos ~3.8). Informational beach intent is won.
3. **Brand is strong.** "apartmani monako igalo" variants: pos 1.0-1.7, CTR 18-26%. The brand funnel works — informational visitors are coming back and searching for you by name.
4. **The commercial gap is the story.** The homepage ranks pos 3.4 for "igalo apartmani" (2,643 impr) but converts only **0.9% CTR = 23 clicks**. "apartmani igalo" (1,633 impr, pos 5.6): 2.4% CTR. "booking igalo apartmani" (555 impr, pos 3.1): 0.2% CTR. At pos 3-4 a normal CTR is 8-12% — you are leaving roughly **150-250 clicks/month** on the table on these three queries alone. The title/meta are already good, so the cause is the SERP itself: Booking, Airbnb and hotel packs with prices, review stars and photos crowd these queries; a plain blue link loses the click even at pos 3.
5. **Improvers worth noting:** "blatna plaza igalo" pos 9.5 → 6.1 (22 → 58 clicks), "plaza igalo" 13.2 → 8.5, "igalo smjestaj" 12.2 → 9.0, atrakcije page 20.4 → 17.1, English beaches page 22.6 → 16.7.

## Recommendations (priority order)

1. **Win the SERP visually on commercial queries — structured data.** Add `VacationRental` (or `Product`/`Offer` + `AggregateRating`) schema to the homepage so price ("od €40") and the ⭐4.9 rating render as rich results. This is the single highest-leverage fix: it attacks the 0.9% CTR at pos 3.4 without needing any ranking gain. Also add `FAQPage` where legitimate FAQs exist on the page.
2. **Harvest the "cene/cijene" intent.** "smjestaj igalo sa cijenama", "apartmani igalo cijene", "blatna plaza igalo smestaj cene" all show. Make sure the homepage displays a clear, crawlable price table per apartment/season (text, not image), and consider a dedicated pricing anchor/section (`#cjenovnik`) linked sitewide.
3. **Convert the beach traffic you already own.** plaze-herceg-novi.html gets 1,766 clicks/28d. Strengthen the in-content CTA to the homepage/booking near the Igalo beach sections (above the fold, and after each Igalo-area beach), with price + phone. Even a 2-3% assisted conversion is more bookings than any new ranking would deliver this month.
4. **Push "igalo" (generic, 1,751 impr, pos 11.5) and "igalo smestaj/smjestaj" (pos ~9-9.5) from page 2 to page 1.** Internal links from the beach pages using anchors "smještaj u Igalu" / "apartmani u Igalu" pointing home; both spelling variants (ekavica/ijekavica) already appear in queries — ensure both exist naturally in on-page copy.
5. **Stara Banja opportunity:** "plaza stara banja igalo" (101 impr, pos 3.0) and "igalo stara banja smestaj" (51 impr, pos 6.3) are new. A short section on the Blatna plaža page (or beaches article) about Stara Banja beach + a smestaj link would lock these in cheaply.
6. **Leave English pages alone for now.** They rank pos 10-17 with minimal volume; ex-YU + diaspora is the market. Revisit only if DE/EN impressions grow.
