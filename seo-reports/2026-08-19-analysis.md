## 8. Method note

The category split in section 2 is the most useful thing these reports have
produced, and it took the YoY rule to see it. Five weeks of month-over-month
comparison showed a site growing 40–96%; the same data cut by year and by
intent shows one half growing 59% and the other losing 47%.

Nothing about the underlying numbers changed. Only the comparison did.
