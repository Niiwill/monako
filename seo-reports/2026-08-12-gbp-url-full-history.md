# The GBP landing URL — complete history, 2026-07-25 → 2026-08-08

**URL:** `https://apartmani-igalo.com/?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=organic`
**Life to date:** 15 days. Verified zero impressions in the 16 months before
Jul 25 (queried back to 2025-04-01). It is the only parameterised URL the
property has ever recorded.

---

## Executive summary

Three things, in order of importance:

1. **This is the best-positioned commercial surface the business has.**
   Position **2.71** overall, **73.7% of its impressions sit at position
   1.0–1.9**, and the query mix is almost purely accommodation intent. The
   organic homepage sits at 8.09 on the same queries. It reached that
   position within two days and has held it flat since — strong, but not
   improving.

2. **It is not a duplicate page and there is nothing to fix technically.**
   URL Inspection returns *"URL is unknown to Google"* for it while `/` is
   indexed with a correct self-canonical. This row is the **local pack /
   Business Profile surface**, not a second indexed URL. No canonicalisation
   problem exists.

3. **Measured in website clicks alone, it has not paid for itself yet — and
   GSC cannot settle that question.** The local pack's primary actions are
   calls and direction requests, which never enter Search Console. The GBP
   export is now the blocking item, not a nice-to-have.

## 4. The CTR question — 0.95% at position 2.71

At position 2.7 an ordinary organic result earns 10–15%. This earns 0.95%.
That is not a broken snippet; **it is what the local pack looks like in
Search Console.**

When someone sees the business in the map pack they can call, get directions,
open photos, read reviews, or visit the website. GSC records the impression
and counts only the last of those as a click. Every other action — including
the phone call, which for this business *is* the conversion — is invisible
here.

Two consequences:

- **The 44 clicks are a floor, not a total.** The last GBP export put calls
  at 6.20/day against 2.9 website clicks/day here. If that ratio still holds,
  the local pack is delivering roughly three times more contact than this
  table shows.
- **There is no snippet fix to make.** Nothing about the meta description or
  title changes local pack behaviour. The levers are the Business Profile
  itself: photos, review velocity, response rate, and the **booking link,
  which is still empty.**

The URL appeared on **Jul 25**, the day a large NAP/schema batch merged:

```
a80f812  Align site NAP and schema with Google Business Profile export
f8cf85c  Fix GBP/website inconsistencies found in the consistency audit
d3ec78d  Fix Search Console vacation rental errors: use LodgingBusiness
2273507  Resolve @id collision and consolidate the business entity
a76bff6  Unify business name across all pages and surface SMS
44b701a  Use postal code 85347 consistently
cc8087f  Re-verify site against 2026-07-26 GBP export
```

That work did what it was meant to do: it tied the website entity to the
Business Profile firmly enough for Google to start surfacing the business,
with its website link, in the local pack.

**The cost is organic demotion on the same queries.** Google does not like
showing one business twice, so the organic homepage was pushed down exactly
where the local pack took over:

The plain homepage `/` overall: **375 → 300 clicks**, 10,319 → 6,313
impressions, position **5.61 → 8.09**.

Read together: **brand queries gained (+20), the two biggest non-brand
commercial terms lost (−11)**, and the net on measurable commercial intent is

The −31 headline therefore does not sit in the commercial queries. It sits in
the tail, where individual queries carry 0–2 clicks and noise dominates. I
would not act on the tail figure.

### Honest limits of this reading

- 15 days is short, and the surface was still climbing at the cut-off.
- Total named query coverage across both URLs actually *rose* (228 → 307), so
  "the homepage lost its long tail" is not supported; the tail clicks moved
  around more than they disappeared.
- **The decisive number is not in GSC at all.** If the local pack lifted
  calls at all, the trade is clearly positive. If calls are flat, it cost ~31
  website clicks per 15 days.

Positions 9.43 and 8.75 are demotions, not losses — if the local pack slot
ever disappears, they should recover. Worth confirming they do not drift
further.

every axis and the sample is still small; the 15-day verdict is provisional.
