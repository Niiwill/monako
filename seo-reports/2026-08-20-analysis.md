## 2. ⭐ Correcting yesterday: both "losses" were noise

### `mesecni-najam-igalo` — not a loss, a win

Yesterday I wrote it was "the biggest loss, 4.00 → 8.14". Here is the full
series:

| Month | Impressions | Clicks | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| April | **2** | 0 | 4.00 |
| May | **3** | 0 | 4.00 |
| June | 7 | 0 | 12.00 |
| **July** | **202** | **13** | 7.80 |
| **Aug (1–17)** | **272** | **8** | 8.19 |

The May "position 4.00" was measured on **three impressions**. The page went
from effectively invisible to 272 impressions and **21 clicks where it had
zero**. Visibility rose ~90×, and average position rose with it because the
page now ranks for many queries instead of one or two.

**This is one of the summer's clearest wins and I filed it as the biggest
loss.**

### `igalo smestaj na obali` — same error

| Period | Impressions | Position |
|---|---|---|
| May | **5** | 2.00 |
| July | 88 | 9.15 |
| Aug | 53 | 10.40 |

Five impressions. There was never a position 2.00 to lose.

### Why this keeps happening

On Aug 16 I wrote: *"state the sample size that would make a movement real
before calling it."* I then applied it correctly to `praznik-mimoze` and the
homepage — both of which I labelled "watch, not alert" — and broke it in the
same report on a May→August comparison, because a four-month gap *feels* like
a big enough sample even when one end of it is three impressions.

**The rule needs a second half:** the sample test applies to *each end* of a
comparison, not to the span between them.

## 7. Method note

Yesterday's report contained a strong, well-evidenced position analysis and
two false findings in the same document, both from the same cause: reading a
position off a three-impression month.

The pattern across this engagement is consistent. Every wrong call has come
from a comparison whose control was weaker than it appeared — a control page
that was not a control, an aggregate that hid a ramp, a baseline built on
three impressions. Every correct call has come from either a pre-registered
threshold or a crawl-date check.

Adding to the standing run:

> **Before quoting a position, print the impressions behind it.** A position
> is a weighted average; under about 50 impressions it is not a measurement.
