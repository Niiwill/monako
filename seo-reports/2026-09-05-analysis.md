## 2. ⭐ Sitemap `lastmod` test — REJECTED

Pre-registered on Aug 21, before the fix shipped:

> *If the pages whose `lastmod` moved show no crawl-date movement by Sep 4,
> record it as no effect and stop citing sitemap hygiene.*

The corrections went live on master **Aug 23**. The control page:

| | Baseline (Aug 23) | **Now (Sep 5)** |
|---|---|---|
| `vesti/kafici-barovi-…html` | 2026-08-01T16:37:06Z | **2026-08-01T16:37:06Z** |

**Identical to the second.** Thirteen days, no recrawl.

The other named page, `monthly-rental-igalo.html`, is not usable as evidence —
I edited it on Sep 2 for an unrelated reason and said at the time that it had
stopped being a clean sample. That leaves one control, and it says nothing
happened.

**Result: no effect.** This was the expected outcome and I wrote it into the
commit message at the time: *"lastmod je nagovještaj koji Google ignoriše kad
se pokaže nepouzdanim. Ovo ispravlja netačan podatak zato što je netačan, bez
očekivanja mjerljivog efekta."*

The change stays — 23 dates that were factually wrong are now factually right,
and that is worth having regardless. **But sitemap hygiene comes off the list
of things I propose as an intervention.** Three of the last five things I have
shipped now have a formally recorded null result.

## 7. Method note

Three closures today, and all three came from the same discipline rather than
from new insight: a threshold written before the data (sitemap), a split by
surface (brand query), and a baseline taken before a change shipped (AdSense).

Worth stating plainly, because it is the honest ledger:

> **Of the interventions this engagement has formally tested, one has a
> positive result** — the entity and content work in June–July that moved
> foreign name variants from positions 25–59 into the top ten. **Four have
> null or negative results**: PR #76, the meta reverts, the image sitemap,
> the atrakcije refocus. **One is now null**: sitemap `lastmod`.

The pages that are working — `plaze`, `blatna-plaza`, the New Year page —
are working because they were built or corrected once and then left alone.
That is the pattern the data supports, and it is the reason today's action
plan is empty.
