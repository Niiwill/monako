`atrakcije` this week: clicks 107 → 60, CTR 4.69% → 3.49%, **position
improved 8.25 → 6.66.** Position 1.6 places better and CTR still down — the

### What this means and what to do

`sitemap.xml` already carries `lastmod 2026-08-10` on all five, which is the
correct signal and has been sent. The constraint is crawl budget, and it is
distributed by internal link strength — which is exactly why the two worst
offenders are the two least-linked pages (`restorani-igalo`, 32 days;
`restaurants-hn` EN, 27 days).

Concrete action: add contextual internal links to `restorani-igalo` and
`restaurants-herceg-novi` from `plaze-herceg-novi` (1,215 clicks/W3, the
site's strongest page). This raises crawl priority and is the same mechanism
the three silent articles need.

**Do not touch these pages' metas again.** Any further edit resets the clock
and makes the measurement unreadable. The correct move is to wait for the
recrawl and read it.

## 7. Method note

Third report in a row where checking `lastCrawlTime` changed the conclusion.
Today it turned "the fix isn't working" into "the fix hasn't shipped to
Google yet" — opposite diagnoses, opposite actions.

Adding to the standing run, alongside the CTR-anomaly sweep and the
never-call-a-turn-from-an-aggregate rule:

> **Before reading any on-page intervention, check `lastCrawlTime` on the
> pages it touched.** A change that Google has not crawled is not a failed
> experiment, it is an unshipped one, and the correct response is a crawl
> signal, not another edit.
