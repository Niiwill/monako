## 2. ⭐ The image channel — I have been reading a composition artifact

For two weeks I have flagged the image position as deteriorating (14.22 →
14.46 over 28d, 17.07 in the last week). Here is what actually happened.

**Image position by page, W2 vs W3:**

| Page | W2 impr / pos | W3 impr / pos |
|---|---|---|
| `plaze-herceg-novi` | 4,135 / 7.84 | 3,277 / **7.38** |
| `10-razloga` | 1,638 / 6.66 | 1,300 / **6.31** |
| `blatna-plaza` | 821 / 13.85 | 718 / **12.31** |
| `atrakcije` | 1,287 / 20.27 | 998 / **19.76** |
| **`rent-a-bike` (EN)** | **35 / 68.94** | **675 / 67.24** |
| `/en/` | — | 279 / 36.53 |

**Every established page improved. The average got worse.**

| | All pages | Excluding the two new entrants |
|---|---|---|
| W2 | 16.34 | 16.05 |
| W3 | **19.58** | **16.52** |

`rent-a-bike-herceg-novi-igalo.html` went from 35 to 675 image impressions at
position 67. That single page moves the site average by three places while
nothing got worse.

**This is exactly the artifact I wrote a rule against on Aug 19** — average
position is impression-weighted, so adding a badly-ranked page degrades it
without anything degrading. I wrote the rule for queries and then failed to
apply it to pages.

**Real image finding, YoY:** established English pages have genuinely lost
ground since last year, and that is not composition —

That is the image problem worth a session of its own. It is not what I have

## 6. Shipped: 23 sitemap `lastmod` corrections

**How I got here, including the wrong turn.** Atrakcije has not been recrawled
since Aug 11 despite changing Aug 17. I checked the sitemap and found its
`lastmod` read `2026-08-10` — apparently the blocker. I wrote a script to
correct every stale date against git.

**The script moved atrakcije backwards, from 2026-08-18 to 2026-08-17.** The
entry was already correct; master had updated it on merge and my working tree
was four commits behind. I reverted that one entry and never move a `lastmod`
backwards.

**So the atrakcije crawl is not blocked by the sitemap.** Google has a correct
Aug 18 signal and has not come back. There is nothing to fix and nothing to do
but wait.

The audit did find 23 genuinely stale entries — most from the Aug 10 entity
batch, which touched nearly every page without touching the sitemap:

| Example | Sitemap said | Actually changed |
|---|---|---|
| `praznik-mimoze-program.html` | 2026-07-26 | 2026-08-10 |
| `en/index.html` | 2026-07-26 | 2026-08-10 |
| `mesecni-najam-igalo.html` | 2026-07-26 | 2026-08-10 |
| `index.html` | 2026-08-10 | 2026-08-15 |

All 23 corrected to their true git dates — never to today's date. XML
validates, 31 URLs intact.

**Honest expectation: probably nothing.** Google treats `lastmod` as a hint
and ignores it when it proves unreliable. This is correcting false data
because it is false, not because I predict a ranking effect. If I claimed
otherwise it would be the fourth unfalsifiable change of this engagement.

**Pre-registered:** if no crawl-date movement appears on those pages within 14
days (by Sep 4), record it as no effect and stop citing sitemap hygiene.

## 8. Method note

Two of today's three headline findings are corrections of my own reporting:
the image channel (composition artifact I had been calling a decline) and the
sitemap (a blocker I diagnosed, shipped against, and then disproved).

The pattern holds. Every wrong call this engagement has come from a comparison
whose control was weaker than it looked. Today the controls that worked were
the same-weeks-last-year page test (§3), the excluded-entrant recomputation
(§2), and the prose-only word count (§4) — each one a second measurement that
could have contradicted the first.

Adding to the standing run:

> **Before reporting a change in average position, recompute it without pages
> or queries that entered or left the set.** If the movement disappears, it
> was composition.
